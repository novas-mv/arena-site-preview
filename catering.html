Who caters, and who supplies whom.

Arena Investments

Institutional catering remains the main business of Arena Investments and Management Services Pvt Ltd. The company holds a shareholding in Paresco Pvt Ltd, held since 2018. Since 2019 Paresco has managed supplies for the Prisons Cooperative Society, and it caters for institutional staff under a five‑year contract awarded in April 2023.

Arena General Trading

Arena General Trading manages supplies for the Arena group companies. These activities serve different business requirements from those of Paresco.
