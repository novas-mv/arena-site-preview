From local business to hospitality.

Before the first guesthouse, the Arena name was associated with small souvenir shops in K. Maafushi.

2011

The first guesthouse

Bunyameen Ahmed proposed converting his wife's house in Maafushi into accommodation for tourists, rather than renting it to local residents. After a three‑month renovation it opened in 2011 as Arena Lodge. Early bookings came through online channels such as Agoda and Booking.com, when local island tourism had limited visibility.

2012–2016

Building visibility and expanding

Positive guest reviews brought visibility. As demand grew, Arena Lodge Sky opened in 2012 as a further guesthouse. The original Arena Beach Hotel opened in Maafushi in 2014, and Arena Lodge Sky was discontinued when Arena Beach opened. Arena Grand followed in June 2016, on land adjoining Arena Beach.

  1. 2008

    Inmate catering began

    Arena Investments began inmate catering, the base of its catering business.

  2. 2011

    Arena Lodge

    The first guesthouse; it opened after a three‑month renovation.

  3. 2012

    Arena Lodge Sky

    A further guesthouse; discontinued when Arena Beach opened.

  4. 2014

    Arena Beach

    The original Arena Beach Hotel opened in Maafushi.

  5. 2016

    Arena Grand

    Opened in June on land adjoining Arena Beach.

  6. 2018

    Arena Fushi

    Opened in October.

  7. 2019

    Paresco operations

    Arena Investments expanded its operations through Paresco, to manage supplies for the Prisons Cooperative Society.

  8. 2020

    Arena Beach redevelopment

    The original hotel was demolished during the pandemic lockdown, beginning the redevelopment.

  9. 2021

    Arena Veli

    Opened in November.

  10. 2023

    The new Arena Beach

    Opened, increasing the existing hotel operation to 200 rooms. In April, Paresco was awarded a five‑year contract to cater for prison staff.

  11. 2024

    Arena Beach Resort project

    Arena leased a lagoon near K. Maafushi in the final quarter; works began shortly afterwards.

  12. 2027–28

    Targeted

    Arena Palm Hiya, target January 2027. Arena V. Thinadhoo, target July 2027. Arena Beach Resort, target end of 2028.
