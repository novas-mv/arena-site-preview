Hospitality, catering and trading in the Maldives.

Three companies under common family ownership. Arena Pvt Ltd runs the hotels and local tourism, Arena Investments the prison catering, and Arena General Trading supplies both.

The group grew from a family business in K. Maafushi into a hotel operation, a prison catering business and a portfolio of new hospitality projects.

200 rooms today, 650 keys planned.

The two hotel projects will bring the hotel portfolio to 500 rooms. Including the 150‑key Arena Beach Resort, the planned operating and development portfolio will reach 650 keys.

Arena InvestmentsCatering and supplyInmate catering since 2008.

Arena Investments began inmate catering in 2008. It has held a shareholding in Paresco Pvt Ltd since 2018. Through Paresco it has managed supplies for the Prisons Cooperative Society since 2019; Paresco also caters for prison staff.

Trading

Catering from 2008, hotels from 2011, 200 rooms by 2023.

  1. 2008

    Inmate catering began

    Arena Investments began inmate catering, the base of its catering business.

  2. 2011

    Arena Lodge

    The first guesthouse; it opened after a three‑month renovation.

  3. 2012

    Arena Lodge Sky

    A further guesthouse; discontinued when Arena Beach opened.

  4. 2014

    Arena Beach

    The original Arena Beach Hotel opened in Maafushi.

  5. 2016

    Arena Grand

    Opened in June on land adjoining Arena Beach.

  6. 2018

    Arena Fushi

    Opened in October.

  7. 2019

    Paresco operations

    Arena Investments expanded its operations through Paresco, to manage supplies for the Prisons Cooperative Society.

  8. 2020

    Arena Beach redevelopment

    The original hotel was demolished during the pandemic lockdown, beginning the redevelopment.

  9. 2021

    Arena Veli

    Opened in November.

  10. 2023

    The new Arena Beach

    Opened, increasing the existing hotel operation to 200 rooms. In April, Paresco was awarded a five‑year contract to cater for prison staff.

  11. 2024

    Arena Beach Resort project

    Arena leased a lagoon near K. Maafushi in the final quarter; works began shortly afterwards.

  12. 2027–28

    Targeted

    Arena Palm Hiya, target January 2027. Arena V. Thinadhoo, target July 2027. Arena Beach Resort, target end of 2028.
