Two generations lead the group.

Bunyameen Ahmed and Zameera Abdul Razzag, and their four children. Their backgrounds span business management, hospitality, finance, retail, project development and human resources.

Directors

Bunyameen Ahmed, Chairman and Owner of Arena Group, established Arena Investments and Management Services Pvt Ltd and began institutional catering in 2008.

In 2011 he initiated the Arena Lodge hospitality venture, proposing to convert his wife's home into guest accommodation.

Zameera Abdul Razzag is a Director and shareholder of each of the three group companies, in hospitality, catering and trading.

She and Bunyameen Ahmed are the parents of Ahmed Samih, Aminath Shiuny, Mohamed Shah and Hassan Sameeh.

Ahmed Samih is the Managing Director of the group companies. His background spans finance, accounting, strategy and business operations.

He holds an MBA specialising in Financial Management from the University of Ballarat, Australia (Malaysia campus), and a Bachelor of Business with double majors in Accounting and Banking & Finance from Victoria University, Australia (Malaysia campus).

Aminath Shiuny combines a business and finance background with hospitality experience.

She holds a Bachelor of Business in Accounting, Banking & Finance through Victoria University's programme at Sunway University, Malaysia. She is actively involved in the group's trading and retail operations, focusing on retail outlets, merchandising, inventory and stock management, procurement coordination, financial control and operational performance.

Mohamed Shah brings more than a decade of leadership experience.

He holds a Bachelor of Quantity Surveying with Honours from Taylor's University, Malaysia, supported by a Foundation in Science from SEGi University College. His responsibilities include project development, construction planning, cost management, procurement and development oversight. He also contributes to event management, food and beverage technical support, new concepts and operational development.

Hassan Sameeh brings ten years of experience in tourism, hospitality and organisational development.

He began his studies at SEGi University College, Malaysia, before graduating in International Tourism Management from Taylor's University, Malaysia. He supports human resource management and development, industry partnerships and professional networks, alongside technology and digital development. His work contributes to Arena's organisational culture, brand image and human capital.
